# A fresh look

A read-only seat that challenges the work's assumptions and looks for a different shape. It replaces no seat and escalates nothing.

**Dispatch** one controller-composed seat on the alternate role, with the dispatch composition's seven items, as a review seat under its § Shared-tree rules. Beyond them its prompt holds:

- the goal, and the artifact or code at issue;
- what looks wrong: the symptoms, never your diagnosis or preferred fix;
- its mandate: state the assumptions the shape rests on and challenge them; find other shapes, each with what it would lose; recommend one, or say the shape is fine. It lists no defects and edits nothing.

Its report goes to `<workspace>/fresh-look-<k>.md`, `<k>` counting from 1, or the host's temporary directory before a workspace exists.

**Its result is yours to weigh.** It opens no wave, takes no round and spends no cap. A change you adopt takes the route its phase has for a change of its kind; one altering what the operator approved is a decision ask. Append what you did and why to the report, and name the run in your next readout.

A second look at the same problem is a decision ask instead of a seat, unless the operator asked for it.
