## Inline mode

You may implement a task directly in the controller session only where it is overdetermined (`skills/subagent-driven-development/references/review-terms.md` § The arrangement questions) and fits your remaining context without pushing out the coordination state you need through the branch review; otherwise dispatch it, the default. Review applies **unchanged**: write your report at the same `sdd/` path, and dispatch the same reviewer with the same plan pointer, four verdicts and gates. A task the plan marks `Review: branch only` keeps that review when you implement it: the mark applies to a dispatched task. **Any loop-entering finding converts the task to standard dispatch** — at any severity — and a fresh implementer owns the fixes. Append `Task <N>: inline` where a dispatched task's implementer line goes (`crucible:subagent-driven-development § The Task Loop`, step 1): every dispute on the task's diff is the adjudicator's (`crucible:subagent-driven-development § Disputes and adjudication`), and after a compaction that line still shows the diff is yours.
