## Finish

1. **Record checks:** inspect the feature workspace against the ledger — the campaign records where their phases ran (round records, readouts, fix lists); the ledger's sections; under `sdd/reports/`, per task, `task-<N>-report.md`, `task-<N>-review.md` (none where the latest run completed branch review only), each wave's `task-<N>-re-review-w<W>.md`, each dispute batch's `task-<N>-adjudication-<b>.md` and each snapshot blessing's `task-<N>-snapshot-<k>.md` — for a task run again, its latest run's, in their `-x<X>` form — and their `-r<R>` forms, and `sdd/reports/lane-report.md` where the lane dispatched an implementer; under `sdd/branch-review/`, each `pass-<k>.md`, `wave-<n>-fix.md`, `wave-<n>-re-review.md` and `adjudication-<b>.md`. List every gap explicitly; never paper over one.
2. **Close readout** to the operator — a message, not a file, its decision asks in a final block, each with options and recommendation. It lists dismissed BLOCKER/MAJOR findings with their rulings; every ruling you made yourself, with its reason — disputes and plan conflicts, the plan patches they led to, the contained amendments, the plan phase's included; each ruled premise finding, with its ruling; every improvement you adopted, named as a lightly held change; each ruling or requested change a fixer declined, as an ask; the lane's departures, the record-check gaps, and, after loop activity that followed an earlier answer, what changed since. Then **the work left open**, each item with its source and your proposal to keep or drop it, and why: parked BLOCKER/MAJOR findings with their rulings, premise asks still unruled, the deferred minors and leads (a RECOMMENDED lead you did not adopt with its verdict; where a cleanup wave ran, why it left each one), and the `## Questions` items not listed above, with your disposition — declined improvements and what earlier phases carried among them. Where the project's instructions name no home for open items and something is left open, ask where it goes or whether to drop it, with the offer to record the answer (`skills/redteaming/references/formats.md` § Open items). Where this feature's landing could complete its umbrella, say what `skills/brainstorming/references/umbrellas.md` § Closing review has the readout say. End with your recommendation and why: one of the options `crucible:finishing-a-development-branch` § Step 4: Present Options offers on this branch, or more work; recommending a pull request, also ask that skill's question whether the feature folder's files go into it. It assembles from `## Adjudication rulings`, `## Deferred minors`, `## Questions`, the `Task <N>: parked` lines and the lane section, following the ledger's predecessor link where one exists.
3. **The answer:** in `crucible:subagent-driven-development § Finish`.
4. **The open items and the umbrella mark:** send the work left open, as the answer left it, where `skills/redteaming/references/formats.md` § Open items sends it: the home the project's instructions name or the answer named, an in-repository one written on this branch, with any instruction-file line the answer recorded in a committed file; the items the answer dropped go nowhere. No item takes a ruling of its own. Where the spec, folded document or lane note carries an `Umbrella:` line, mark this feature's entry in that umbrella `landed`, in the form `skills/brainstorming/references/umbrella-children.md` § Marks states. A feature with no line marks too where an umbrella on the base branch holds an unmarked entry opening with its slug (`git grep -n "<feature-slug>" <base-branch> -- "*/umbrella.md"`) and its feature folder was born on a tree without that umbrella — `git cat-file -e <commit>:<umbrella-path>` fails for its folder's first commit, the last line of `git log --diff-filter=A --format=%h -- <feature-folder>`: it was in flight when that umbrella was written. A feature born on a tree that held the umbrella is not that entry's child. Where its tree lacks that umbrella, it merges the base branch in first, never rebasing. A feature no umbrella names writes no mark; an entry already marked needs nothing. Commit what this step wrote on this branch, so the suite runs on the tree that lands; where the loop reopens, the next answer rewrites the items written here.
5. **The suite:** run the full suite, or the project's declared merge gate, on HEAD, and show the operator a green run's summary line. Do not classify a red run: it is a BLOCKER finding against the branch, handed to `crucible:requesting-code-review`'s loop and counted against its cap, the wave's fix base being the HEAD the suite ran on. That a failure is not the branch's — it fails on the merge base too, or is environmental — is a dispute like any other (`crucible:subagent-driven-development § Disputes and adjudication`), which you may raise before the wave opens. A failure ruled not the branch's, whoever ruled, is recorded with its ruling and not raised again while it fails the same way; show the operator the red run with that ruling: only an answer that accepts it lets finishing proceed past its Step 1.
6. **Delete the feature workspace:** delete `.crucible/<feature-slug>/` and nothing else, and report to the operator anything you could not delete.
7. Then use `crucible:finishing-a-development-branch`, which carries out the answer without presenting its menu again and asks only what the answer left open.
