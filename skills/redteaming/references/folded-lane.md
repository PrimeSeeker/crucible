## The folded lane

The folded preset **folds the plan into the spec**: one combined spec+plan document, `folded.md` in the feature folder, and **no plan phase**. The spec campaign in `crucible:redteaming` applies **unchanged** — panels sized by the existing dials, the lens roster, the stop rule, the cap, blind verification and the terminal pass; only the artifact and the handoff differ.

**Entry is not yours to declare.** The fold is proposed at brainstorming's present-the-design step (`crucible:brainstorming` § Proposing the arrangement (step 4)) and **confirmed or vetoed by the operator there**, before any artifact is written; the confirmation is the route entry in the brainstorm decision record, which reaches you in the workspace. Read it there.

**The round loop** gains three lines:

- **Round 0's pre-pass also covers the plan half**: ownership closure and the overlap arithmetic over the task list, and the set census of the plan phase's pre-pass (`skills/redteaming/seat-prompt.md`, its pre-pass passage), its inputs widened to the repository's tracked text for it.
- **After every fix wave**, the terminal pass's included, you re-run the liveness, ownership and overlap arithmetic yourself, booking no dispatch, and record it in that record's `Verifier block`, or under its `Fix list` where it has none.
- **The round-1 seat carries the right-sizing and outside-the-frame lenses** alongside general.

**Marks.** A task of the combined document may carry `Review: branch only` (its form: `crucible:writing-plans` § Per-task structure — four fields, all required); the close readout lists the marked tasks (`crucible:redteaming` § Round readout). Once the campaign has closed, no mark is added.

**Deferral owners.** A fix item that lands a `Deferred to implementation:` marker — round 0's and the terminal pass's wave included — places it in its owning task or, in the spec half, names it in the Contract of each owning task, in the same item; the ownership closure, at round 0's pre-pass and your re-run after every wave, checks that each marker has an owner.

**Records are standard.** The workspace and the record formats are unchanged; the absent plan-phase record is the fold, not a gap.

**Unfolding.** Where the campaign or execution shows the work needs a separate plan — your judgment — undo the fold as an **operator decision ask on the supersede path** (`crucible:redteaming` § Flip paths — load-bearing content mid-campaign or mid-execution): a spec-only successor and a separate plan, each with its standard phase, the ledger carrying the predecessor link. Both go into the existing feature folder as `design.md` and `plan.md`, beside `folded.md`, which stays as the forensic record (`formats.md` § Feature folder). There is no dedicated ejection protocol.

**Exit and execution.** The close hands off **directly to SDD** and owns the ledger seeding, open premise asks, `deferred-minor` entries and what earlier phases left open included (`crucible:redteaming` § Exit). SDD and the branch review run with standard caps and inputs; the plan pointer names the combined document, so seats read the whole of it, as designed.
