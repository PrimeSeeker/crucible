## Premise findings

A premise finding claims that the request, what the Intent asks for (for a child, its umbrella's too; on the very-small lane, the lane note's), or a firm decision is wrong, and carries the label `premise`. Your side, at any phase, the spec campaign included:

**A mislabel.** You correct a finding's label by the definition above, in either direction, your reason recorded with it: a finding labelled `premise` that the definition classes as ordinary — against a lightly held decision, say — is merged or routed as an ordinary finding, and an unlabelled finding against the request, what the Intent asks for or a firm decision takes the label and this section's route.

**The ask.** Every premise finding goes to the operator as a decision ask, carrying the severity its seat staked for the operator's weighing — never a change you confirm, whatever `skills/redteaming/references/flip-paths.md` § The containment test would find. It is an open decision ask, not an open BLOCKER or MAJOR: by itself it holds open no gate keyed on one — the cap escalation, the branch review's end condition, task completion — opens no fix wave and consumes no wave cap. The readouts carry it until it is ruled, and while it is open, work goes on.

**The ruling.** What the operator's ruling brings takes the route any change of its kind takes: in a spec campaign, the next fix list, where a change that re-shapes a major mechanism makes overturning one after code reasonably expected again, and its round no stop round (`crucible:redteaming` § Stop rule and terminal pass); from that campaign's close, the paths of `skills/redteaming/references/flip-paths.md`, the ruling standing as the operator's confirmation. In execution, a ruling given during the task loop lands as a plan change at the next task boundary (`crucible:subagent-driven-development` § Plan changes mid-execution), the affected tasks not yet dispatched waiting for it; one given later, at the branch review or its close readout, lands in a branch-review wave, counted against its cap like any, and Finish restarts after it (`crucible:subagent-driven-development` § Finish). A ruling that keeps the decision opens no wave.

**Precedence.** A finding that is both a premise finding and a binding item found wrong — a firm decision, a lane note's Intent — takes this section's route. Any other binding item found wrong keeps its own: in its spec's own campaign the fix list, from that campaign's close `skills/redteaming/references/flip-paths.md` § The containment test, and from an implementer a BLOCKED report with a proposed correction.

**Carrying an open ask.** Until the ledger exists, an open premise ask stays in the records and close readouts of the phase it is open in, and SDD's Setup seeds it into the ledger as open (`skills/subagent-driven-development/references/setup.md`); once the ledger exists, write it under its `## Adjudication rulings` directly and tell the operator of it in a one-line message, not waiting for the close readout; one still open when an umbrella lands goes as `skills/brainstorming/references/umbrellas.md` § Landing and `skills/brainstorming/references/umbrella-children.md` § Children state.
