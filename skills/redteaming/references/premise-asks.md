## Premise findings

A premise finding claims that the request, what the Intent asks for (for a child, its umbrella's too; on the very-small lane, the lane note's), or a firm decision is wrong, and carries the label `premise`. At any phase, correct a finding's label by this definition, in either direction, recording why with the finding: one labelled `premise` that does not fit (against a lightly held decision, say) is merged or routed as ordinary; an unlabelled one that fits takes the label and this section's route.

**The ask.** Every premise finding is a decision ask, carrying the severity its seat staked for the operator's weighing — never a change you confirm, whatever `skills/redteaming/references/flip-paths.md` § The containment test would find. It is not an open BLOCKER or MAJOR: by itself it holds open no gate keyed on one (the cap escalation, the branch review's end condition, task completion), opens no fix wave and consumes no wave cap. The readouts carry it until it is ruled; meanwhile work goes on.

**The ruling.** The ruling's change routes as any change of its kind:

- in a spec campaign, the next fix list; if the change re-shapes a major mechanism, overturning one after code is reasonably expected again and its round is no stop round;
- from that campaign's close, the `flip-paths.md` paths, the ruling as the operator's confirmation;
- during the task loop, a plan change at the next task boundary, affected undispatched tasks waiting for it;
- at the branch review or its close readout, a branch-review wave counted against its cap, Finish restarting after it.

A ruling that keeps the decision opens no wave.

**Precedence.** A premise finding against a binding item (a firm decision, a lane note's Intent) routes here. Other binding items found wrong keep theirs: in their spec's campaign, the fix list; from its close, the containment test; from an implementer, a BLOCKED report proposing a correction.

**Carrying an open ask.** Until the ledger exists, an open premise ask stays in the records and close readouts of the phase it is open in, and SDD's Setup seeds it into the ledger as open. Once the ledger exists, write each premise ask directly under its `## Adjudication rulings` and tell the operator in one line, not waiting for the close readout. One still open when an umbrella lands goes as `skills/brainstorming/references/umbrellas.md` § Landing and `skills/brainstorming/references/umbrella-children.md` § Children state.
