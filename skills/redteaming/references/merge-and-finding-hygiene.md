## Merge and finding hygiene

When all seats return (state `returned`), merge into `## Merged finding list`, one entry per finding, shaped as `round-record.md` § Round record format, field 5. Every finding and question is dispositioned at merge, none discarded silently: every raw seat finding, a lost seat's that reached disk included, is merged into an entry or closed as a duplicate of one, and listed in that entry's `origins:`. Record a ruling for every partly-applied, parked or rejected finding, a partly-applied one's naming the part not acted on. An entry whose fix reverses an earlier round's applied fix names that round and entry id in its substance.

A seat finding labelled `premise` merges into an entry labelled `premise` (field 5), disposition `parked`, its ruling a decision ask to the operator, routed, carried and relabelled as `premise-asks.md` § Premise findings states.

**Merge from the indexes.** Every seat report opens with a `## Index`, one line per finding (id, severity, one-sentence claim, evidence pointer), an advisory aid nothing checks against the findings. Open a finding's body only to author its fix, to rule on a contested finding or a collision between seats, or where its index line is not enough to rule. When you open a body to author its fix, check each of its sub-points against the fix item; add an unaddressed one to the item or rule on it in the entry. For a BLOCKER or MAJOR entry the check covers every origin's body, duplicates included; outside that, a duplicate of a finding you have read, a MINOR entry's duplicates and a MINOR you defer are ruled from their index lines.

A finding of any severity whose resolution needs local mechanism (inside one implementer's natural scope) is `deferred-to-implementation`. Non-local mechanism, as `crucible:writing-plans` § Deferring detail lists it, is never deferred: it lands in the artifact's Interfaces & boundaries (a plan's Inter-task contracts). A finding a fix list takes whose resolution corrects or deletes existing artifact text is `applied`, since a shipped artifact is never silently edited; it stays `applied` when only its local mechanism is deferred, on a marker in the same fix-list item. A deferred finding stays visible: it keeps its row in the merged list; its `Deferred to implementation:` marker gets an owning task (same section), which the pre-pass's ownership closure checks from plan time on; and the round readout carries the round's deferred count. A deferral covering binding content or non-local mechanism, or a misplaced one, is a finding against it; a recorded deferral's missing detail is not (same section).

Route an accepted MINOR that no fix list takes (`fix-application.md`) by the first that applies: (1) where it names missing non-local mechanism, a fix-list item into Interfaces & boundaries; (2) where it needs local mechanism, `deferred-to-implementation`; (3) where it is a design judgment for the operator, `parked`; (4) otherwise `deferred-minor`, left unfixed in the artifact, which reaches the branch review's triage through the ledger SDD's Setup seeds (`skills/subagent-driven-development/references/setup.md`) or an umbrella's landing (`skills/brainstorming/references/umbrellas.md` § Landing). A later round's finding repeating a `deferred-minor` closes as its duplicate, in an entry naming that round and entry, and is seeded once. A MINOR you find wrong is `rejected`.

Read seat questions in full, and give every question id a disposition in `## Question dispositions` (field 5). A question doubting the request, what the Intent asks for or a firm decision is always carried to readout: the operator settles it, not you, the Intent's author. Round 0's questions, answered in its readout, follow the same rule. **Adopted** is for an improvement you take as a lightly held change, named among the readout's technical calls: it rides this round's fix wave as a fix-list item naming the question id and never opens a wave by itself; in a round with no wave, it is carried to readout instead.

**Finding hygiene** and finding scope bind every seat through its template, and you enforce both at merge. Hygiene: a staked severity on each finding, a BLOCKER without evidence a question, and a run transcript or `UNVERIFIED` on each code-shaped claim. `UNVERIFIED` findings never enter a fix list as-is: verify or discard them, the outcome on an indented continuation line of the entry.

**Finding scope** (`skills/subagent-driven-development/references/review-terms.md` § Finding scope; at any locality, a binding item itself wrong and an unrecorded consequential choice are findings): a finding outside it is still merged and accounted for, and `rejected` with that ruling.
