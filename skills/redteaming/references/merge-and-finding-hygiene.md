## Merge and finding hygiene

When all seats return (state `returned`), merge into the round record's `## Merged finding list`, one entry per finding in the shape `round-record.md` § Round record format, field 5 fixes — its `origins:` element naming every per-seat finding id it accounts for, and its disposition from that field's vocabulary, **with a ruling recorded for every partly-applied, parked or rejected finding** (a partly-applied one's naming the part not acted on). Silent discard is forbidden; `crucible:redteaming` § Round readout says which of them the readout carries.

**Premise findings.** A seat finding labelled `premise` merges into an entry carrying that label after its severity (`round-record.md` § Round record format, field 5), disposition `parked`, its ruling a decision ask to the operator, routed, carried and relabelled as `skills/redteaming/references/flip-paths.md` § Premise findings states.

**Merge from the indexes.** Every seat report opens with a `## Index` — one line per finding: id, severity, a one-sentence claim, an evidence pointer — an advisory merge aid that nothing checks against the findings. Read the indexes first and merge from them; open a finding's body only to author its fix, to rule on a contested finding or a collision between seats, or where its index line is not enough to rule. **When you open a body to author its fix, check each sub-point of it against the fix item**: an unaddressed sub-point is added to the item or ruled in the entry. For a BLOCKER or MAJOR entry the check covers the body of every origin, duplicates included; outside that, a duplicate of a finding you have read, a MINOR entry's duplicates and a MINOR you defer are ruled from their index lines. Questions are read in full. A merged entry whose fix reverses an earlier round's applied fix names that round and entry id in its substance.

**Deferral** (`deferred-to-implementation`) is the disposition, **at any severity**, of a finding whose resolution needs mechanism that is **local** — inside one implementer's natural scope. Non-local mechanism, as `crucible:writing-plans` § Deferring detail lists it, is never deferred: it lands in the artifact's Interfaces & boundaries (a plan's Inter-task contracts). A finding a fix list takes whose resolution corrects or deletes existing artifact text is `applied` — corrections land at artifact stage, because a shipped artifact is never silently edited — and stays `applied` when only its local mechanism is deferred, that mechanism riding a marker in the same fix-list item. A deferred finding stays visible three ways: it **keeps its row** in the merged list; its `Deferred to implementation:` marker gets an owning task (`crucible:writing-plans` § Deferring detail), which from plan time on the pre-pass's ownership closure checks; and the round readout carries the round's **deferred count**. A deferral covering binding content or non-local mechanism, or a misplaced one, is a finding against it; a recorded deferral's missing detail is none (the same section).

**A MINOR no fix list takes** (`crucible:redteaming` § Fix application and blind verification) that you accept takes, in this order: the artifact's Interfaces & boundaries, by a fix-list item, where it names missing non-local mechanism; `deferred-to-implementation` where its resolution needs local mechanism; `parked` where it is a design judgment for the operator; otherwise **`deferred-minor`**: left unfixed in the artifact, it reaches the branch review's triage through the ledger SDD's Setup seeds (`skills/subagent-driven-development/references/setup.md`), or an umbrella's landing (`skills/brainstorming/references/umbrellas.md` § Landing). A later round's finding repeating a `deferred-minor` closes as its duplicate, in an entry naming that round and entry, and is seeded once. A MINOR you find wrong or outside the finding scope stays `rejected`.

**Every finding and question is dispositioned at merge.** Every raw seat finding is merged into an entry, or closed as a duplicate of one and carried in the absorbing entry's `origins:` element; a lost seat's findings that reached disk are merged like any other's. Every seat question id gets a disposition in the record's `## Question dispositions` (`round-record.md` § Round record format, field 5). **A question that doubts the request, what the Intent asks for or a firm decision is always carried to readout**: the operator settles such a doubt, not you, the Intent's author. Round 0's questions, answered in its readout, follow the same rule. **Adopted** is for an improvement you take as a lightly held change, named among the readout's technical calls: it rides this round's fix wave as a fix-list item naming the question id, and never opens a wave by itself; where the round has no wave, the improvement is carried to readout instead.

**Finding hygiene** binds every seat through its template, and you enforce it at merge: a staked severity on each finding, a BLOCKER without evidence a question, and a run transcript or `UNVERIFIED` on each code-shaped claim. **`UNVERIFIED` findings never enter a fix list as-is** — verify or discard them, the outcome on an indented continuation line of the entry.

**Finding scope** (`skills/subagent-driven-development/references/review-terms.md` § Finding scope; at any locality, a binding item itself wrong and an unrecorded consequential choice are findings) binds every seat through its template, and you enforce it at merge: a finding raised outside it is still merged and accounted for, and dispositioned `rejected` with that ruling.
