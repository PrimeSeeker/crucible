## The plan phase — one verification round

A plan gets **one verification round**, recorded as the single record `campaigns/plan/round-01` — no plan `round-00`, no plan `terminal-pass/`. The phase **never loops**: one fix wave at most, no round cap, no cap arithmetic. A plan finding that shows the spec wrong takes the flip path against the spec (`crucible:redteaming` § Flip paths — load-bearing content mid-campaign or mid-execution), never another plan round; a contained amendment lands in this phase's wave (below).

**The seats.** Four dispatches; the pre-pass and the coherence seat(s) run in parallel against one frozen plan revision:

1. **Mechanical pre-pass** (one agent, instrument `redteam-seat`): liveness (every declared mechanism has a producer and a consumer), ownership closure, the overlap arithmetic and the set census (`skills/redteaming/seat-prompt.md`, its pre-pass passage), its supporting inputs widened to the repository's tracked text for it — **run it, don't read it**. Its report, `campaigns/plan/round-01/seat-p0.md`, is a seat report wherever seat reports count, so it carries `## Index`, `## Findings` and `## Questions` like any seat's; its arithmetic goes in `## Worksheet`, before `## Findings` (slot card `worksheet` selector `pre-pass`).
2. **Coherence seat** (instrument `redteam-seat`) — one, reading spec and plan **together**, full-scope; **two for a large plan** (your judgment, lightly held; roughly mid-teens tasks or unusually high interface density is the advisory proxy).
3. **Blind verifier** (instrument `blind-verifier`) on the fix wave, under its unchanged input contract; its fix-list scope is the coherence seat's items.
4. **Terminal pass** (instrument `terminal-pass`, `phase` set `plan`) — one fresh seat after the fix wave, scoped to this single round.

**Every dispatch follows `crucible:redteaming` § Panels and § Dispatch composition** — model, effort, roster and filled slot card — and the tree check in `crucible:redteaming` § Inputs and state. **Announce the phase shape** — seats, lenses, dispatch count — in the phase-start readout and proceed; the announcement is not an approval gate.

**Marks are plan content.** Where a task carries `Review: branch only` (its form: `crucible:writing-plans` § Per-task structure — four fields, all required), the coherence seats grade each mark, by their mandate reminder (`skills/redteaming/seat-prompt.slots.md`); the close readout lists the marked tasks (`crucible:redteaming` § Round readout). No seat grading marks reads this phase's fix wave or a repair, so neither adds a mark; a question proposing one is answered or carried (`crucible:redteaming` § Merge and finding hygiene).

**One merged wave.** Pre-pass and coherence findings are merged under the spec campaign's finding hygiene, disposition and merge rules, and the wave's fix list takes what `crucible:redteaming` § Fix application and blind verification states. A fix item for a `deferred-to-implementation` finding lands its marker in the plan and appends that marker's routing-table rows — one per owning task, the source cell naming the plan site, owner assigned — so the close seeds them like any other row.

**A contained amendment** — a correction to the spec that `skills/redteaming/references/flip-paths.md` § The containment test finds contained — lands in the same wave as an item of the record's `## Fix list` opening `Contained amendment:`, naming the binding item and the change and, on an indented continuation line once it is made, the amendment commit. Its reason, and why it is contained, go in the ruling of the merged entry that led to it, never in the item: the fix list is the blind verifier's input. The verifier sees only the item's plan-side hunks, the re-pin and any plan patch; you confirm the amendment commit landed, in the `Verifier block`. The phase readout names it among its technical calls.

**Post-wave verification, in order:** you re-run the pre-pass's liveness, ownership and overlap arithmetic, not its set census, over the amended plan yourself, booking no dispatch, and record it in the `Verifier block`; the blind verifier checks the wave; then the terminal pass performs its two mandates under their ordering (`skills/redteaming/terminal-pass-prompt.md`).

**Two sanctioned repair paths, neither a loop.** A NOT-LANDED verdict permits **one scoped repair edit** and a re-check **of that item only**, by the verifier whose verdict it was — never a new wave. A terminal-pass finding a fix list would take takes the same path, its other MINORs the dispositions of `crucible:redteaming` § Merge and finding hygiene. After a repair on either path, re-run the arithmetic over the repaired plan yourself, again booking no dispatch. Neither path opens a wave or books a dispatch beyond its own.

**The second read, at most one,** follows the repair of a terminal-pass finding and, record-only, the cases `crucible:redteaming` § Stop rule and terminal pass names; one read covers both. Record the close under the record's `## Terminal pass` first, then resume the pass with a message setting its `follow-up` selector to `second-read` and naming a new report file, `campaigns/plan/round-01/second-read.md`; after a repair it re-reads **full-scope over the repaired region**, not the flagged item alone. Deliver the close after it, its corrections added. Of what is left open then, a MINOR not against binding text takes `deferred-minor`; the rest goes to the operator as a **decision ask**.

**Dispatch arithmetic:** at most 4 (pre-pass, coherence seat, blind verifier, terminal pass), 5 with two coherence seats, plus the verifier's re-check and the second read where they run — up to two.

**A degraded round is not a loop:** a lost seat is re-dispatched under the host-confirmed top-up rule (`crucible:redteaming` § Panels); the quorum block is kept for lost seats, and cap arithmetic does not apply.

**The record.** One record, the plan-phase record of `formats.md` § Round record format, its states running through `confirmed`, written last. The terminal pass's report lands as `campaigns/plan/round-01/terminal-pass.md` and its second read as `second-read.md`; the pass's `Record corrections: <N>` outcome goes under the record's `## Terminal pass`.

**Close.** After the terminal pass, any repair and any second read, and before the handoff, **seed the SDD ledger's deferred list from the plan's deferred-items routing table** — one entry per row, carrying the item's id, its one-line intent and its owning task — **seed each `Contained amendment:` item of the fix list under the ledger's `## Adjudication rulings`** as one of your own rulings, in that item's form with the reason and why it is contained from its merged entry's ruling beside it, and **seed each open premise ask there as open** — those open in this record and any the spec campaign's close carried (`skills/redteaming/references/flip-paths.md` § Premise findings). **Seed each `deferred-minor` entry** of the spec campaign's records and this one under the ledger's `## Deferred minors`, once, as `<source>: minor (deferred): <one-liner>`, `<source>` being `Spec` for the spec campaign's and `Plan` for this record's, for the branch review's triage.

**What earlier phases left open.** At this close, and at the other closes that seed the ledger (the folded campaign's, and on the very-small lane SDD's Setup), seed under the ledger's `## Questions`, one line each as `<phase>: <item> — carried`, `<phase>` being `brainstorm`, `spec` or `plan`, what the brainstorm and every phase since, the closing one included, left open: dispositions that send work to a follow-up outside the feature (not `deferred-to-implementation`, which the routing table routes, nor `deferred-minor`, seeded under `## Deferred minors` above), parked entries the operator has not ruled on, the unaddressed part of partly-applied ones, and carried questions the operator left unanswered. Read them from the brainstorm decision record and the round records. Premise asks keep their own carrying, above.
